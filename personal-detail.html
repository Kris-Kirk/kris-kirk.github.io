Cedar Strip Paddleboard

Woodworking

Overview

I hand-built a hollow wooden stand-up paddleboard from scratch using a stitch-and-glue technique for the internal skeleton and bead-and-cove cedar strips for the hull. The deck features a custom pattern created by alternating Western Red Cedar tones.

Fabrication Process

  • Frame: Cut marine plywood ribs and aligned them on a strongback for stability.
  • Hull: Milled thousands of linear feet of cedar strips and glued them individually.
  • Finishing: Applied layers of fiberglass cloth and epoxy resin, followed by marine spar varnish for UV protection.

ISS Experiment (SSEP)

Student Spaceflight Experiments Program

Overview

I led a student team that won a competitive bid to send a microgravity experiment to the International Space Station (ISS). Our research investigated the viability of composting red-worms in a zero-gravity environment to support future sustainable space travel.

Key Achievements

  • Proposal Writing: Authored the winning technical proposal selected from hundreds of entries.
  • Fundraising: Secured corporate sponsorship to fund the mission launch fees.
  • Presentation: Invited to present our findings at the Smithsonian National Air and Space Museum in Washington, D.C.

Custom Smart Car Rack

Metal Fabrication

Overview

Designed and built a custom aluminum rack for a Smart Car to carry skis and bikes. The challenge was creating a secure mounting system for a vehicle that does not support standard roof racks.

Fabrication

  • Design: CAD modeling to ensure clearance for the rear hatch and proper load distribution.
  • Build: Cut, bent, and bolted aluminum tubing to create a lightweight but rigid frame.
