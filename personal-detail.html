Cedar Strip Paddleboard

I built this from scratch using a stitch-and-glue technique. I used plywood for the internal ribs and cut Western Red Cedar strips for the deck, using two colors to generate a custom pattern.

After extensive sanding, I fiberglassed the board using epoxy and cloth. This project taught me how to use planers, jointers, and how to thicken epoxy using glass bubbles.

Student Spaceflight Experiment (SSEP)

I led a group that won a bid to send an experiment to the International Space Station. We investigated growing composting red-worms in microgravity.

Our experiment launched on a SpaceX Falcon 9 in Feb 2017. I learned how to write professional proposals, raise funds from corporate sponsors, and present at the Smithsonian Air and Space Museum.

Custom Smart Car Rack

I used aluminum extruded square tubes to create a custom ski/snowboard rack that fastens to the car's towing hook ports.
